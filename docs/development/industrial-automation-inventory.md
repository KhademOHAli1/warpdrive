# Industrial automation inventory and production

## Summary

Warpdrive will deliver full functional coverage of InvenTree and Shelf, with the two capability sets integrated into one workspace for industrial automation and instrumentation. The first release lets Sales check actual availability on a CRM deal, place a single-item order and resolve a shortage through assigned Projects work, a completed build and a recorded shipment. The [parity register](inventory-shelf-inventree-parity.md) defines reference baselines, current evidence, and acceptance for the full programme.

## Owners and outcomes

| Role | Hand-off and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Sales | Qualifies the inquiry in CRM, confirms the order and communicates availability and lead times. |
| Engineering and R&D | Define part numbers, electrical and mechanical revisions, approved alternatives and release evidence. |
| Procurement | Sources approved parts and manages order confirmations, inbound dates and supplier discrepancies. |
| Warehouse | Receives, counts, transfers, reserves, picks and ships material at named locations. |
| Production planning and operators | Schedule jobs, assign work, kit material, build and record output. |
| Quality | Approves receiving and release, records electrical and functional test evidence and owns nonconformance disposition. |
| Asset custodians | Manage custody, bookings and calibration for tools, jigs and field equipment. |
| Finance and service | Own financial posting, warranty terms, field support and return dispositions. |

Initial acceptance is scenario-based. Sales can navigate from an authorized deal to availability and confirm a line without retyping the customer. A tested shortage produces one build and one set of assigned native to-dos after retries. Shipment requires enough reserved stock, and every quantity change reconciles with the ledger. Measure median stock-check time, shortage response time, inventory accuracy, first-pass yield and on-time shipment during the pilot. Use measured baselines before setting operational service targets.

## Operating model and architecture

1. **Inquiry and promise.** CRM records customer, contacts, product requirement, quantity and requested date. An authorized deal link carries the canonical deal ID into Inventory. Compare demand with sellable on-hand minus reservations. Confirmation reserves eligible stock and records remaining demand. Future phases add quotes, price tiers, tax and currency, delivery schedules, substitutions and date-aware available-to-promise.
2. **Engineering release.** A part number links to immutable releases and effectivity. Each release should reference the design BOM and manufacturing BOM, STEP/enclosure model and drawing, PCB schematic/layout and Gerber or ODB++ fabrication pack, pick-and-place and assembly instructions, firmware and checksums, nameplate/label artwork, inspection plan, calibration instructions and declarations. Model files as versioned artifacts with purpose, checksum, author, approval, secure object ID and an auditable release bundle. Engineering change orders capture where-used, disposition of old stock and substitutes. For the MVP, track SKU revision and the committed BOM. The controlled file lifecycle belongs in a subsequent release.
3. **Planning and supply.** Convert shortages into gross requirements, net usable stock, then procurement, subcontract or child builds using approved lead times and capacity. Freeze the relevant BOM for each build. Later planning includes reorder/min-max, safety stock, forecast netting, pegging, lot-sizing, planner exceptions, work center calendars and finite scheduling. Receipts can be partial, quarantined or rejected, with lot, supplier, cost, country of origin and test evidence.
4. **Physical work.** Pick and reserve materials, issue them, build, test and release finished units to sellable stock. Projects handles planner/operator/quality tasks and records their completion. Inventory posts reservations, component consumption and outputs in its own transaction after completion conditions are met. Later station records cover WIP, cycle time, serialized genealogy, recipe/firmware versions, test artifacts, rework, scrap, yields and commissioning.
5. **Fulfillment and after-sales.** Pick reserved stock, print controlled labels and packing lists, record carrier and tracking, and ship. Post shipment to accounting via reconciled integration with a durable receipt. Retain customer/serial traceability for returns, warranties, recalls and replacement. Keep return receipt, quarantine, repair and commercial credit as distinct transactions.
6. **Equipment and service.** Tools, reference instruments, test jigs and loaner equipment have a responsible custodian, planned windows, handover and return. Calibration due/failed state blocks scheduling where applicable. Crib audits, consumables, technician dispatch and customer-installed equipment link back to the relevant job, site, calibration certificate, or as-built serial.

CRM continues to own deal visibility. The Inventory API checks deal access again for attached orders. Existing Work Profiles and CRM Activities remain the homes for client delivery workflows and customer calendar follow-ups. An Inventory production template assigns native Projects work items to active members of an authorized internal project. Project access continues to be checked independently. Task text excludes customer names, prices and contact data. Opening an order never grants access to its production project.

Inventory owns reservation, physical quantity and append-only movement effects in PostgreSQL. Reserve material under row locks keyed by stock identity, aggregate component demand, and lock in deterministic order. Orders pin production quantities, selections and BOM contents at confirmation. Use version checks for state transitions and one receipt per confirmation intent. Commit the order, stock reservation and SQL-only task creation in the same transaction when possible. Future network integrations can use durable outbox receipts with fenced retries. Keep generic worker integrations additive and expose explicit versioned events when a second consumer needs them.

### Managed CAD and EDA intake (ERP-01)

The Releases tab accepts a versioned exporter manifest and its named files for the selected product and draft revision. The manifest identifies the exporter version, source revision, file role, size, and SHA-256. The first workflow covers a Gerber and drill ZIP, PCB source, placement, a STEP enclosure component, manufacturing BOM, firmware, label artwork, and test instructions. The BOM uses Warpdrive’s manufacturing SKU and quantity mapping. The original local proof bundle uses KiCad 10.0.5 for the fabrication archive, placement export, and STEP spacer. [KiCad board file format](https://dev-docs.kicad.org/en/file-formats/sexpr-pcb/index.html) · [KiCad CLI](https://docs.kicad.org/10.0/en/cli/cli.html)

An intake job owns the manifest, upload references, and retry history. The existing release owns its artifacts, BOM, approval, and build pins. Core issues private Silo forms for the exact staged files. The registered Worker verifies their size, type, SHA-256 and release-specific contents, then rechecks the submitting editor and draft before publishing the complete bundle and BOM source in one transaction. A retry names the current failed attempt. Approval uses the existing release action.

The qualification uses a separate draft alongside incumbent Rev A and Rev B releases, compares byte hashes and build pins, and exercises upgrade and independent Web rollback. ### Engineering change control and effectivity (ERP-02)

Add change review to the product’s existing Releases tab. Use the currently effective released package as the baseline and an exact draft of the same item as the candidate. The review shows design-file changes and manufacturing BOM deltas, approved alternatives from the BOM, and existing where-used references. It binds the submitted review to the candidate’s file digests and resolved BOM, so a revised package must be reviewed again. InvenTree is the reference for preserving references to older revisions, comparing BOMs and invalidating validation after BOM edits. [InvenTree part revisions](https://github.com/inventree/InvenTree/blob/67229e49308256def6b01806a2ee0cd81f52a7dc/docs/docs/part/revision.md) · [InvenTree BOM validation and comparison](https://github.com/inventree/InvenTree/blob/67229e49308256def6b01806a2ee0cd81f52a7dc/docs/docs/manufacturing/bom.md#bom-validation)

Use the existing product editor and approver grants for submission and decision. An approver can return a review with a reason or approve the exact package for a scheduled UTC cutover. Record the baseline, candidate, reviewed digest and attributed decisions. The production and CRM entry points for new builds must resolve the same effective release inside the existing product lock. Pending changes and scheduled releases stay out of new-build selection until the cutover. Older builds, operation replays and sealed subassembly references retain their original release IDs and BOMs. Products without change control retain their existing release behavior. [Release approval](../../src/features/inventory/releases/service.ts) · [CRM builds](../../src/features/inventory/service.ts) · [Production builds](../../src/features/inventory/production/service.ts)

Show disposition only for positions traced to the previous release’s production output in sites the viewer can access. A warehouse editor can hold or release eligible positions using existing stock state validation and ledger receipts. Positions with reserved quantities or unrelated holds require their owning workflow. Manual inventory that cannot be traced to the approved release has unknown origin. Reviewers work with existing release files and the manufacturing BOM while a standalone structured design BOM, rework and scrap execution, and serial-based effectivity require separate increments. [Production origin](../../src/features/inventory/warehouse/productionOrigin.ts) · [Stock state changes](../../src/features/inventory/warehouse/changes.ts)
