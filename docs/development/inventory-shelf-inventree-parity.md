# InvenTree and Shelf functional parity

Warpdrive's target is full functional coverage of **both** products, integrated with CRM, Projects, and the industrial automation production workflow. The [Inventory MVP](industrial-automation-inventory.md#delivery) provides the first verified transaction path. Manufacturing release files and build pinning have local qualification under REL-03 through REL-06. The entire register remains the delivery scope.

## Reference baselines and acceptance

- **INVT-01.** The [InvenTree 1.7.3 feature audit](inventree-1.7.3-feature-audit.tsv) maps 100 scenarios across Core, Web, REST, plugins and mobile to the [pinned source](https://github.com/inventree/InvenTree/tree/67229e49308256def6b01806a2ee0cd81f52a7dc) and [official documentation](https://docs.inventree.org/en/stable/). IT-100 tracks native app and physical hardware qualification.
- **SHELF-01.** The [Shelf 2.6.0 feature audit](shelf-2.6.0-feature-audit.tsv) maps 100 scenarios across the [pinned source](https://github.com/Shelf-nu/shelf.nu/tree/66dea8d079a1e8e6a65563a3632c55725bde3c76) and published hosted, paid, enterprise and mobile capabilities. It draws on the [official feature comparison](https://www.shelf.nu/pricing#compare), [knowledge base](https://www.shelf.nu/knowledge-base) and [Companion workflows](https://www.shelf.nu/mobile-app). SH-069, SH-083 and SH-094 track hosted audit entitlements, enterprise sign-in and session behavior, and the scope of public integrations.
- **Evidence.** Each audit row identifies a workflow, applicable actor and client, source, current Warpdrive coverage, owning backlog task and an acceptance case. `tagged_source` is inspection of the pinned source. `official_docs` identifies published behavior that still needs runtime verification. `open_reference` identifies behavior or availability that needs clarification. The family summaries below provide planning context. PARITY-01 and PARITY-02 require end-to-end evidence across authorization, lifecycle transitions, failure and retry, and supported data exchange.

Inventory owns movements and consumption of sellable material. Assets owns custody and return of reusable equipment. Both use shared identities, sites and locations, with a logged transition when stock is commissioned as an asset. CRM owns customer and supplier relationships. Projects owns assigned work and its permissions. Engineering owns approved releases, and jobs link to the release that applies to the build.

## Shared sites and locations (PLAT-01)

InvenTree models one parent/child stock location tree, with structural locations, external locations and descendant stock rollups. Shelf keeps a workspace-owned hierarchy and stores location references separately across asset placement, kits, custody and consumption. A Shelf quantity pool can span several locations. [InvenTree stock location model](https://github.com/inventree/InvenTree/blob/67229e49308256def6b01806a2ee0cd81f52a7dc/src/backend/InvenTree/stock/models.py#L128-L326) · [Shelf data model](https://github.com/Shelf-nu/shelf.nu/blob/66dea8d079a1e8e6a65563a3632c55725bde3c76/packages/database/prisma/schema.prisma#L934) · [Shelf hierarchy rules](https://github.com/Shelf-nu/shelf.nu/blob/66dea8d079a1e8e6a65563a3632c55725bde3c76/apps/webapp/app/modules/location/service.server.ts#L824)

- **Shared directory.** A site is a first-class identity. Each location has a stable ID, a site-scoped code and an optional parent at that site. Rename and reparent preserve IDs. Archive preserves historical references and requires archiving active children first. Restore requires an active site and parent. CRM organizations keep their independent customer and supplier identities.
- **Domain ownership.** Inventory points stock positions at directory IDs. Assets will point placements, custody and kits at those same IDs. Each domain defines placement eligibility and authorizes its own operations. Commissioning stock as equipment requires a logged handoff with both references. Existing SKU-level quantities remain Unlocated until an administrator reconciles free units to a location.
- **Access.** Core serves both directory views from the same tables. Administrators manage sites and access. Accepted staff see their granted sites. Viewers can read them and managers can also edit locations within them. The same session loses access after revocation on its next read or command. Successful command retries recheck the current grant and return a fresh projection.
- **Delivery order.** PLAT-01-WEB and PLAT-01-DIR have local qualification. The persisted directory unblocks Inventory placement and the Assets register. Close PLAT-01 after scoped access and a logged commissioning handoff are exercised with persisted Inventory and Assets records.

The Web checkpoint at `27d0bd00` is available in the loopback demo at `http://127.0.0.1:3090/locations`. Authenticated Chrome created a site and nested bin, moved and renamed the bin across contexts with its ID intact, denied parent archive with an active child, restored the bin and verified desktop and 390 px layouts. Fixture edits reset on reload and the browser attempted zero business writes. The [backlog](warpdrive-operations-backlog.tsv) links the fixture receipt.

The integrated checkpoint at `d9571535` qualified live Web and Core with native PostgreSQL 16. Migration `0129_shared_locations` advanced a populated installation from 129 to 130 migration receipts. A rerun kept the same receipt count and rows. The PostgreSQL suite preserved existing Inventory balances, reservations, order and movement history, and the approved Rev A release and BOM pinned to an open build. Chrome verified both contexts, site-specific grants, denied cross-site writes, actor and CSRF checks, persistence across reload, and revocation in the same session. The commissioning handoff remains part of PLAT-01.

## Part master and warehouse stock (INVT-02 and INVT-04)

- **Part master.** Migration `0130_inventory_catalog` binds existing inventory items to part families and retains their item IDs and stock SKUs. Additional revisions share the part number and have separate stock identities. The Part master Web and Core surfaces support hierarchical categories, templates and variants, typed parameter inheritance and compatible-unit conversion, lifecycle states, notes, tags, related parts and scoped files. The populated 0129 to 0130 upgrade and live Chrome catalog run preserved incumbent stock and verified creation, search and revision identity.
- **Warehouse stock.** Migration `0131_inventory_warehouse` adds scoped stock positions, order allocations, incoming plans, location policies and trace events. Receipts can carry a lot, serial, expiry, state and owner. A tracked receipt records one unit per serial, unique within its part revision. Eligible orders reserve the earliest-expiring eligible bins first and fall back to free Unlocated stock. Shipments and consumption settle recorded allocations. Incoming, held, quarantined, expired, customer-owned and external quantities are excluded from available stock. Stock transactions use whole units.
- **Follow-on boundaries.** INVT-03 and INVT-07 own suppliers, purchasing and linked inbound orders. INVT-09 owns serialized manufacturing output. Existing build output remains Unlocated when the workflow supplies no destination or serial.

The live warehouse checkpoint at `b47fbcf1` passed on PostgreSQL 16 after applying `0131_inventory_warehouse` to populated 0130 stock. The browser confirmed the legacy 19 on-hand and 4 reserved units, explicit bin assignment, a Rev B serialized receipt after reload, scoped access, structural placement rules, separate inbound counts and quarantine. The PostgreSQL suite verified exact positioned reservations, shipping and cancellation, and retained orders, movement history and pinned Rev A build data. The [operations backlog](warpdrive-operations-backlog.tsv) links the local receipts.

## Warehouse operations (INVT-05)

- **Movement history.** Warehouse operations joins positioned events with the Unlocated remainder of historical aggregate movements. It keeps event locations captured at the time of movement, even after a serial changes bins. Filters cover part, date, location, event type, lot and serial. Site readers see local deltas. Transfer references and reasons are shared with readers who can see both endpoints and all parts on the order.
- **Transfer orders.** A draft names the source bin, destination bin and exact stock positions. Dispatch checks the current free quantity and moves it into transit. Partial receipts add actual units at the destination. Lots retain their metadata and serialized units retain their stock identity. Managers need write access at both endpoints. Drafts can be cancelled. Once dispatched, stock is received and can be sent back in a return transfer.
- **Stock corrections.** Record the counted quantity and reason on a position. The command checks the current count and reservation floor. Reversal adds a compensating entry linked to the original and requires the stock to remain at the recorded location without a subsequent physical change. Separate split and merge actions and installation and disassembly remain under INVT-05-STRUCT. Full stocktake workflows remain under INVT-06.

The local checkpoint at `4c68c01a` passed 27 PostgreSQL integration tests and 16 unit tests. Live authenticated Chrome and Core ran against PostgreSQL 16.10 after upgrading from populated `0131_inventory_warehouse` to `0132_inventory_warehouse_operations`. Reapplying the migration preserved all 133 receipts. Chrome verified dispatch and partial receipt, site-scoped history, and correction and reversal. The integration suite also verified same-site bin transfers, competing dispatches and fulfillment of the incumbent sales reservation. The upgrade retained 19 on-hand, 4 reserved and 15 available units, original lot and serial records, and allocation, order and movement history. The [operations backlog](warpdrive-operations-backlog.tsv) links the local evidence.
