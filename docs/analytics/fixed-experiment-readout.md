Both report absolute treatment-minus-control risk differences across all randomized eligible units, including units with qualified non-exposure. Holdout control requires qualified absence of exposure. A/B control can have a matching control exposure. No output changes campaign or delivery state.

## Interpretation

For each endpoint the estimated risk difference uses the observed arm proportions. With realized arm sizes `nT` and `nC`, the radius is `sqrt((1/nT + 1/nC) * ln(2/0.025) / 2)`. This applies Hoeffding’s bounded two-sample mean inequality to both tails and clips the interval to the possible risk-difference range `[-1, 1]`. The two endpoint error budgets of 0.025 sum to 0.05 by the union bound, including when the two endpoints within a unit are dependent. [Hoeffding (1963), Theorem 2 and its two-sample corollary](https://www.cs.rpi.edu/academics/courses/spring06/random/hoefding.pdf)

The coverage target is the expected assignment effect for the eligible-unit generating population, conditional on realized arm sizes. It assumes independently sampled, exchangeable units, common-probability Bernoulli assignment, consistency, no interference, complete nondifferential measurement, and enrollment and stopping independent of observed results. Finite-roster randomization inference requires a different procedure. The bound is conservative, especially with few units or an imbalanced split. A primary criterion is demonstrated when its lower bound reaches the predeclared minimum lift. A guardrail criterion is demonstrated when its upper bound stays within the predeclared maximum adverse increase. Intervals straddling a criterion are inconclusive.

The joint coverage statement covers exactly these two endpoints at one predeclared look under the stated assumptions. The collector must attest the analysis history and withhold a confirmatory readout if it cannot verify the single look. The baseline does not handle clusters, geo designs, repeated looks, many variants, covariate adjustment, continuous endpoints or exposure-conditioned effects. These remain admission requirements before any publication.
