# Private settled-window customer economics

`settled-eligible-account-economics-v1` accepts one closed financial window in one currency. The window includes its start and excludes its end. Source settlement covers that end and precedes or equals the observation cutoff. Each posting binds an exact recognized instant and a custody observation at or before the cutoff. One frame binds the account identity, report scope, revenue policy and delivery cost policy.

The collector supplies a complete, authorized, canonical account roster and independently controlled totals for both lanes. The kernel reconciles the exact eligible account set, candidate count, known-account counts, posting counts and subtotals. A posting belongs to one canonical account and one lane. Reused posting keys and duplicate account rows invalidate the result. Identity merges, aliasing, corrections, source permissions and allocation validity must be established in custody before this projection. If any eligible membership is withheld or unresolved, the kernel withholds the entire result.

Realized net revenue is the sum of qualified revenue postings minus qualified credits. The concentration denominator sums only positive net revenue across eligible accounts after netting each account separately. Largest and top-five shares are exact fractions with no account identities. Known zero and negative nets remain in economic totals and are counted separately. Missing revenue prevents a concentration denominator. Costs remain a separate sourced lane. The contribution amount subtracts attributable delivery cost from realized net revenue, and contribution margin divides that amount by positive net revenue. A known zero comes from a complete zero observation. A missing lane exposes only the reconciled known subtotal and coverage, with no full contribution estimate.

The governed input contract distinguishes commerce orders, purchases and refunds, billing contracts, invoices, payments and credits, and delivery-cost observations. Finance must select one qualified recognition policy, resolve overlapping commerce and billing representations, and supply canonical recognized postings. Settled delivery costs require established account attribution and an authorized recognition instant. Costs spanning accounts or periods need separately qualified source allocations before this kernel. CRM won value, purchase counts and raw billing statuses carry no authority as realized revenue. The contribution covers sourced delivery costs only. Corporate profit, allocation, renewal, causal LTV and source or public DTO qualification remain separate work.
