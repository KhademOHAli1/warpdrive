# Qualification frameworks: research and preview boundary

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Research input for approved SP-05.1, SP-05.2 and
SP-05.4, not completion of those tasks or a legal clearance. Sources below are
framework providers, IBM, WIPO and an identified court opinion. Definitions and
recommendations are original paraphrases; no training scripts, question banks,
vendor scorecards, diagrams, exercises or certification material are included.

## Concept mapping

These approaches have different jobs. Qualification categories, conversation
methods and a selling system must not become interchangeable stage lists or
claims of predicted success. The suggested evidence areas are Warpdrive design
proposals, not vendor-approved implementations.

### MEDDPICC's 2026 naming development

The [court opinion in MEDDICC Ltd v. 01 Consulting LLC, No. 24-1836,
document 99, filed 21 April 2026](https://meddicc.com/hubfs/Summary%20Judgment%20Opinion%20-%20MEDDICC-C.pdf)
finds MEDDPICC generic for business-education services/content and supports
cancellation of the disputed US registration (printed pages 31 to 32). This is a
court-authored primary document hosted by a litigant, not a live registry search.
MEDDICC also publishes an [announcement dated 23 April 2026](https://meddicc.com/resources/meddpicc-trademark-cancelled-federal-court-ruling).
MEDDIC Academy's still-published trademark claim must not be repeated as settled
current ownership, and the announcement's broad marketing language must not be
treated as worldwide permission. Appeal/stay history, live registry status,
software-specific scope and relevant non-US territories were not established by
this review. Proprietary courses, scorecards and software remain a separate issue.

## Concrete scope while persistence awaits the owner cut

The current [stage-only template contract](template-contract.md) accepts pipeline
and stage declarations with empty field-reference, rule and action arrays. Its
successful result remains preview-only with installation unavailable. The
[storage coordination request](sales-storage-coordination-request.md) proposes
pipeline/template receipts and mappings; it does not allocate qualification
evidence storage. This research adds neither schema nor runtime support.

Within that source boundary, the next permissible engineering proposal is a
separate, bounded, data-only qualification research catalog and pure preview. It
must not inject qualification definitions into the existing stage manifest or
pretend unsupported capabilities are stage names. Suggested limits and behavior
below are proposals for review, not existing exports or legally cleared scope:

1. **Catalog metadata:** stable entry key, content version, author/reviewer,
   source URLs and access date, original-copy provenance, rights-review status
   and a bounded list of neutral evidence concepts. Keep branded references in
   research metadata pending the specific rights decision; no logos, badges,
   vendor assets or implication of endorsement. Do not auto-fetch sources.
2. **Neutral examples:** use original entries such as buying readiness,
   complex purchase review and customer discovery notes. Sample evidence is
   synthetic. A preview may explain which facts a user could later record and
   show unknown/unsupported results. It cannot select a framework on a persisted
   pipeline, install fields, save customer evidence, enforce stages or trigger
   anything. Keep the existing blank/basic stage paths available.
3. **Later evidence contract:** distinguish unknown, supported, contradicted,
   disputed and not-applicable assertions. Confidence is an explicit assessment,
   not proof. Each assertion needs source reference, author, reviewer, observed
   and reviewed times, freshness policy/version and independent field/source
   visibility. Missing dates do not imply fresh evidence; missing information
   is never silently converted to a negative answer. These remain SP-05.2
   requirements until native authorization and storage are reviewed.
4. **Explainable review proposals:** separate completeness from risk. A rule
   version must identify required evidence, applicability, freshness and how
   unknown/contradictory facts affect its result. Show contributing evidence and
   reasons only when permitted. If required evidence cannot be safely assessed,
   return unsupported/unknown without exposing hidden existence, counts or
   values through a score. Do not produce a win probability, vendor-certified
   score or an automatic block on stage changes. These are SP-05.4 design inputs,
   not executable policies in schema version 1.
5. **Before installation:** obtain the storage owner's allocation and exact
   supported lineage; review durable version/provenance binding, per-pipeline
   opt-in selection, independent evidence access, retention/deletion and audit.
   An approved rights record must state the reviewed copy, naming, languages,
   distribution and any written grant or other accepted basis, including open
   gaps. Recheck current terms and applicable territories for a branded release.
   No schema reservation, source review or this document establishes runtime
   qualification, publication permission or task acceptance.

Recommended immediate decision: retain all six references as researched concepts,
author neutral original evidence guidance and keep any catalog preview data-only.
Do not mark SP-05.1's lawful-boundary acceptance criterion satisfied until its
specific rights gaps are resolved; SP-05.2 and SP-05.4 also remain open.
