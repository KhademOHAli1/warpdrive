## Structural limit of this review

The same-project receipt FK is a useful proposed invariant, but proves neither matching actor/operation/intent nor atomic command execution. The optional fifth-store composite FK requires its commented history `(id, project_id)` unique pair and both non-null columns. It prevents the described cross-project linkage if correctly implemented; its PK supplies at most one payload per event, not mandatory payload insertion or disposition. The joint revision/null predicates were read without finding an additional contradiction beyond the packet's explicit open contracts. No PostgreSQL behavior is established by that source inspection.

Complete operation/member-history facts, title UTF-16 versus SQL length parity, native authority and participating writer fences, accepted combined lineage and allocator approval remain blockers independently of this matrix. No Agency allocation or extension of a MAIL-only reservation is made here.

Future acceptance requires actual owner APIs and a concrete selected representation, followed by source-bound tests for domain/AAD substitution, unavailable/rotated keys, changed-intent conflict, current-authority recovery, deletion/tombstone/restore replay, same-project and required-payload rollback, identifier dependency errors and safe projections. Applicable physical/provider/backup evidence must remain separate from source tests. This document supplies none of those executed qualifications.
