# Language, region, and appearance

Choose English, Iranian Persian (Farsi), or German in **Settings > Language & region**
(`/settings/regional`). Your account saves the language, and the page previews your
date, time, and number formats.

## Calendar and timezone

Set your account time zone in **Language & region**. With Farsi selected, open
**Customize formats** to choose the Iranian Solar Hijri or Gregorian calendar.
The activities calendar and shared date pickers use the selected calendar.

Timed activities preserve an instant; all-day activities preserve a calendar date. Date grouping and composition use the account timezone. A change in display calendar does not reschedule a stored appointment. Review ambiguous or skipped local times when editing around daylight-saving transitions.

The display calendar controls how you read and enter dates. Review the schedule
and time zone on a workflow or calendar event before saving it.

## Digits, numbers, and currencies

Under **Customize formats**, choose Persian or Latin digits when the number
display control is available. Numeric inputs accept supported regional digits
and separators. For CSV files, choose the applicable import conventions in the
[import wizard](/features/import/).

With Iran selected as your country, choose rial or toman display under
**Customize formats**. One toman is ten rials. This preference leaves the
record's currency as IRR and its stored value in rials. Reports keep different
currencies separate.

## Appearance and navigation

Use **Settings > Appearance** (`/settings/appearance`) for the available theme and display options. The sidebar supports expanded, collapsed, and narrow-screen navigation. Search, quick creation, account controls, and notifications are in the shared top bar.

Farsi uses right-to-left layout and the locally bundled Vazirmatn font. Latin interfaces use the locally bundled Inter font. Email addresses, URLs, and technical identifiers keep their left-to-right reading direction inside Farsi content.

**Settings > Workflow preferences** (`/settings/workflow`) contains personal CRM
preferences, including when to show follow-up prompts. Use [Visual workflows](/features/automations/)
to learn about the workflow builder.

## Reporting a language or layout issue

Include the route, interface language, selected calendar/timezone, theme, and action that failed. Use synthetic records or redact account/customer data in screenshots. A useful report includes the intended meaning; a literal word replacement can miss the underlying workflow.
